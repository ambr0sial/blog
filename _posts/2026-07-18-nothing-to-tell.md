people ask you to say something sometimes, like "tell a story, say a thing you did" and everyone has stuff for that. a night, smth stupid they did at fifteen, a thing that went wrong. i just have nothing. i sit there looking for something and theres nothing

i was on my pc. thats the whole thing lol thats all of it. not one year, not two. my whole childhood, all my teens, the entire time where you're supposed to be getting the stuff that makes a life. i was in my room in front of my screen and i thought it was fine. i thought it would start later. idk i genuinely thought it was gonna wait for me

it didnt wait. it just went. it doesnt come back
and i wasn't even bored, thats the part nobody gets. i was having a great time tbh well most of the time. thousands of hours and i got nothing out of any of them. no story, no photo, nothing. i was alive that whole time and i cant show anyone anything

but okay heres the thing i keep thinking about lately. maybe i did have stuff. maybe there were a few things. probably there were?? like statistically there has to be some. a night somewhere, a day that was actually good, someone said smth to me that mattered. but my memory is broken and been broken for years. everything just goes. someone tells me smth and its gone in a week. whole months gone. people bring up stuff we did together and i nod and smile like i remember because technically i was there, my body was there, bbut theres nothing that comes up

so even if i lived a little i cant get to it. its the same as never doing anything but worse ngl, because never doing anything is at least simple. this is maybe having done things and losing them anyways

and yeah if i ever have kids (probably not) they're gonna ask. kids always ask. "what did you do when you were my age" and i'll just sit there. i'll make a joke and change the subject, im very good at that already

people will go "its never too late, go out, sign up for smth, make new memories". i know. thats not the point. i can live from now on, i cant be sixteen again. whats missing isnt in front of me, its behind me, and you cant go build stuff behind you. and lwk whats the point of making new ones when i lose everything anyway. i'd just be doing the same thing with extra steps

some days i feel like im just not made for anything. its how it feels most of the time. im not going anywhere and i was never going anywhere
idk how to end this.

this blog wont just be venting i just need ideas and projects to talk about but yeah

i guess this is the one thing i can actually tell. wrote it down so i cant forget that one at least
